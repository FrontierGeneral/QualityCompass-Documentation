# Nexus Frontier — Support Service Level Agreement

**Effective Date:** March 15, 2026
**Last Updated:** March 15, 2026

---

## Overview

Nexus Frontier is committed to providing reliable, responsive support for all applications published on the Atlassian Marketplace. This Service Level Agreement (SLA) defines our support commitments, response times, and escalation procedures.

## Support Channels

- **Primary:** [support@nexusfrontier.net](mailto:support@nexusfrontier.net)
- **Marketplace:** Via the Atlassian Marketplace support tab on each app listing
- **Documentation:** Available at each app's documentation portal

## Business Hours

Monday through Friday, 09:00–18:00 WET/WEST (Western European Time).
Excluding Portuguese national holidays.

Critical severity issues are monitored outside business hours on a best-effort basis.

## Severity Levels and Response Times

| Severity | Definition | Initial Response | Update Frequency |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|------------------|
| **Critical** | Application is unavailable or a core function is completely broken for all users. No workaround exists. | 4 business hours | Every 4 hours until mitigated |
| **High** | A major feature is significantly impaired. A workaround may exist but is not sustainable. | 8 business hours | Every 8 hours until mitigated |
| **Medium** | A feature is not working as expected but the impact is limited. A reasonable workaround exists. | 24 business hours | Every 2 business days |
| **Low** | A minor issue, cosmetic defect, general question, or feature request. | 48 business hours | As progress is made |

**Initial response** means the first substantive reply acknowledging the issue and providing either a resolution, a workaround, or a clear next step. Automated acknowledgements do not count as initial response.

## Resolution Targets

| Severity | Target Resolution |
|----------|------------------|
| Critical | 24 business hours |
| High | 3 business days |
| Medium | 10 business days |
| Low | Best effort |

Resolution targets are goals, not guarantees. Complex issues may require longer investigation. In such cases, we will communicate updated timelines proactively.

## Escalation Process

If you are not satisfied with the support response or timeline:

1. **First escalation:** Reply to your existing support thread requesting escalation and stating the business impact.
2. **Second escalation:** Email [support@nexusfrontier.net](mailto:support@nexusfrontier.net) with subject line "ESCALATION: [Your Issue Summary]" and include your original ticket reference.
3. **Atlassian Marketplace:** You may also raise concerns through the Atlassian Marketplace support channel for the relevant app.

## Scope

This SLA covers:
- Bug reports and defects in published Marketplace applications
- Configuration and setup assistance
- Questions about application functionality
- Compatibility issues with supported Atlassian products

This SLA does not cover:
- Custom development or feature requests (handled on a best-effort basis)
- Issues caused by third-party applications or unsupported configurations
- Atlassian platform outages or issues outside our control

## Availability

Our Marketplace applications are hosted on Atlassian Forge, which runs on Atlassian's managed infrastructure. Application availability is governed by [Atlassian's Cloud SLA](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/sla). We do not independently guarantee uptime as we do not control the hosting infrastructure.

We commit to:
- Responding to Atlassian-reported incidents affecting our applications within 4 business hours
- Publishing status updates for known issues
- Maintaining application compatibility with Atlassian's supported platform versions

## Changes to This SLA

We may update this SLA to reflect changes in our support capabilities. Material changes will be communicated via our Marketplace listings. The latest version is always available at this URL.
