# Nexus Frontier

We build Forge-native tools that bring operational intelligence to Atlassian products.

Our focus: the problems hiding in plain sight — quality gaps, evaluation inconsistency, performance blind spots — solved with structured processes and deep analytics, entirely within the Atlassian ecosystem.

**Our engineering principle:** Your data never leaves Atlassian's infrastructure. No external services, no third-party dependencies. Secure, reliable tools built on the platform your team already trusts.

---

## Products

### Quality Compass

Structured ticket quality evaluation for Jira. Define criteria, score tickets, track agent and evaluator performance, detect scoring bias, enforce coverage targets, and export analytics — all from inside Jira.
